# Affiliate pricing
- Closing-fee editors share ClosingFeeSlider; explicit partner slider changes convert to fixed ILS, but unchanged stored reward type/value must round-trip without conversion. Publisher agreement percentages retain property-price semantics. Individual live caps use contact_options.lead_daily_limits; pricing snapshots remain immutable. Why: avoid corrupting saved fees or confusing percentage semantics.
- Partner pricing dialogs reuse the publisher reward editor only for an authored active-workspace row; other viewers use redacted public data, with referral discounts inside and removal in trailing table actions. Discount controls require the authenticated referral affiliate; the RPC enforces the same ownership, never publisher authority. Why: preserve tenant isolation and distinct publisher/partner controls.

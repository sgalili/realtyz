- Property filters reuse publisher amenity controls in filter mode and shared option constants; chosen values match authorized listing facts, never guessed missing values. Why: identical menus without changing publication or reward behavior.
- Advanced reward filters retain per-tier thresholds in the existing account/workspace-scoped filter state; positive thresholds drive OR matching at each tier's own minimum, while expansion is presentation-only and slider labels stay independent of potential estimates. Why: apply prices without unscoped caches or cross-tier threshold confusion.
- Property pricing information uses distinct lead cards and a desktop max-w-2xl dialog; the publisher pricing-only editor shares the same bounded presentation without changing wizard or authorization flows. Why: consistent readable pricing across guest and authorized views.
- Closing-split disclosures reuse ClosingSplitNote and existing reward-rate helpers; public closing notes mount only inside existing server-authorized pricing gates. Why: consistent gross/net explanations without changing payouts or visibility.
- The unlicensed-partner closing-commission sentence is shared only from UnlicensedPartnerCommissionNote and mounts as a quiet footnote in the master pricing window and both legal pages, never as a banner or a per-page copy. Why: one legal wording without drift.
- RewardIcons delegate every click to the surrounding master pricing trigger; denied closing details render ClosingLicenseInfo inline there, never a nested dialog. Role/owner/admin privileges never authorize browsing amounts; publisher configuration remains separately authorized. Why: one pricing window without closing-data leaks.
- Reward drafts are keyed by authenticated user, active workspace and listing; reward writes include active workspace predicates. Why: tenant isolation.
- Closing-fee editors share ClosingFeeSlider; explicit partner slider changes convert to fixed ILS, but unchanged stored reward type/value must round-trip without conversion. Publisher agreement percentages retain property-price semantics. Individual live caps use contact_options.lead_daily_limits; pricing snapshots remain immutable. Why: avoid corrupting saved fees or confusing percentage semantics.
- Partner pricing dialogs reuse the publisher reward editor only for an authored active-workspace row; other viewers use redacted public data, with referral discounts inside and removal in trailing table actions. Discount controls require the authenticated referral affiliate; the RPC enforces the same ownership, never publisher authority. Why: preserve tenant isolation and distinct publisher/partner controls.

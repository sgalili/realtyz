---
name: Full repost editor
description: Continue/repost always opens a fully editable post with explicit approval, and cancellation sits at the right edge.
type: feature
---
Continue/repost from any campaign or post status must open the comprehensive editor prefilled with existing content, first comment, groups, schedule and media. All of these are editable. Explicit approval can publish again to selected groups even after completion. The action is “אישור ופרסום מחדש”; normal publication confirmation is “אישור פרסום”. Place “ביטול” at the right edge on the same row as approval. Cancelling must not change the original post or its queue.
